About BookSwap

BookSwap is a student-first platform designed to make sharing and finding books easier than ever. Whether you're done with a textbook or looking for a new read, BookSwap lets you:

• Snap a photo of the book you want to give away

• Send a request to connect with the book owner

•The Search for the book you need using smart filters

•other user can accept or reject the exchange request

features

our features & services

Easy Book Listing & Browsing

•Users log in to list books with title, author, and verified images.
•Books can be browsed by genre and filters through a simple interface.
•Each listing shows availability and helps users discover new reads easily.

Swap Request & Approval

•Users request swaps by offering their own books.
•Owners can accept or reject requests instantly.
•Accepted swaps securely share delivery info for mailing.
•All swap statuses are tracked from the user dashboard.

Post-Swap Chat & Coordination

•An in-app chat opens after swap acceptance for delivery coordination.
•Chat ensures privacy and smooth communication.
•Users confirm shipment, complete the exchange, and leave feedback.
